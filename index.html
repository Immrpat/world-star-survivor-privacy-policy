World Star Survivor Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy covers World Star Survivor for Android (com.worldstarsurvivor.game) and its browser version. The game is developed by Thanapat Wongchalermthan.

Game data stored on your device

The game stores progress, earned rewards, your hero collection and upgrades, your selected team and game preferences so you can pick up where you left off. It also stores a checkpoint when you continue after defeating a Warden. Android keeps the save in app-private storage. Browser play uses the site's local storage and may cache game files for future visits.

The game has no accounts, developer-operated cloud saves, ads or analytics. It does not send saved progress or gameplay reports to the developer or share them with third parties. A report you choose to export is generated on your device. If you share an exported file, its recipients and any service you use to share it can access it.

Network requests

The Android app loads its packaged game files on the device. If you play the web version, your browser requests files from the web host. If you visit the public copy of this policy, GitHub Pages serves it. These hosts may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser information, under their own policies. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details on GitHub's handling.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until it is replaced or cleared. To remove it on Android, go to Settings → Apps → World Star Survivor → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. In a browser, clear the game's site data in your browser settings. These steps remove progress and preferences. Delete any exported reports separately from wherever you saved them.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions or requests, email the developer at pat23.w@gmail.com. If you contact the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond and kept as long as needed to resolve your request. Please share only the information needed to answer your question.
